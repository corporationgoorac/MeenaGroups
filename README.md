## 📖 Overview

The **Meena Groups Enterprise Dashboard** is a centralized, secure internal platform designed to manage the company's core financial and retail operations. It provides a complete ecosystem for tracking customer EMIs, managing retail inventory (Furniture & Electronics), and overseeing complex Chit Fund operations.

Built for scale, this dashboard streamlines daily financial tasks, reduces manual entry errors, and provides real-time analytics on cash flow and pending dues.

---

## ✨ Core Modules

### 💰 EMI & Loan Management
* **Active EMIs:** Track all active customer EMIs with automated due date highlighting.
* **Payment Collection:** Process incoming payments, generate digital receipts, and calculate late-fee penalties automatically.
* **Defaulter Tracking:** Instantly flag accounts that are past due for collection agent follow-ups.

### 🛋️ Retail & Inventory (Furniture & Electronics)
* **Stock Tracking:** Real-time inventory levels for all electronic appliances and furniture assets.
* **Direct Sales to EMI:** Seamlessly convert a retail purchase directly into a structured EMI plan for the customer.
* **Supplier Management:** Track wholesale orders, vendor payments, and incoming stock shipments.

### 🏦 Chit Fund Operations
* **Group Management:** Create and track individual chit fund groups, participant lists, and monthly contribution status.
* **Auction & Dividends:** Record monthly auction winners, calculate dividend splits, and automatically distribute credits to members.
* **Payout Tracking:** Secure logging of final payouts and guarantor documentation.

### 📊 Analytics & Reporting
* **Cash Flow Dashboard:** Daily, weekly, and monthly revenue collection reports.
* **Asset Value:** Current value of active loans vs. physical retail stock.
* **Staff Performance:** Track collections and sales made by individual employees.

---

## 🛠️ System Architecture

* **Frontend:** React / HTML5 (Web-based Administrative Portal)
* **Backend:** Node.js / Express.js Enterprise Engine
* **Database:** Secure NoSQL/SQL Architecture for Financial Ledgers
* **Authentication:** Role-Based Access Control (Admin, Collection Agent, Store Manager)
